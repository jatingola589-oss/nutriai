🍎 Healthy Recipes

Simple and tasty food ideas for everyday meals.

🥣 1. Vegetable Poha

⏱️ 15 minutes Approx. 250 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flattened rice (poha)
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Mustard seeds and curry leaves
  • Small amount of oil, lemon and spices

Preparation

  1. Wash poha gently and drain.
  2. Cook mustard seeds and curry leaves in a little oil.
  3. Add vegetables and cook until tender.
  4. Add poha and spices, mix gently.
  5. Finish with lemon juice.

🥗 2. Sprouts Salad

⏱️ 10 minutes Approx. 180 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup properly cooked sprouts
  • Chopped tomato and cucumber
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and mild spices

Preparation

  1. Cook sprouts thoroughly.
  2. Allow them to cool.
  3. Mix with chopped vegetables.
  4. Add lemon juice and seasoning.

🍛 3. Vegetable Khichdi

⏱️ 25 minutes Approx. 300 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • Rice and moong dal
  • Carrot, peas and other vegetables
  • Turmeric and cumin
  • Water and a little oil or ghee

Preparation

  1. Wash rice and dal.
  2. Add vegetables and spices.
  3. Cook with water until soft.
  4. Serve warm with curd if desired.

🥛 4. Banana Milk Smoothie

⏱️ 5 minutes Approx. 220 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 medium banana
  • 1 glass milk
  • A pinch of cardamom

Preparation

  1. Wash and peel the banana.
  2. Blend with milk and cardamom.
  3. Serve fresh.

* Calories are approximate and depend on ingredients and serving size. Check food allergies before preparing a recipe. Use pasteurised milk.
