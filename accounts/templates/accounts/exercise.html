🏋️ Exercise Plan

Simple movement ideas for everyday wellness.

🚶 Walking

Enjoy a comfortable walk at a pace that feels right for you.

Duration: Start with a few comfortable minutes, as appropriate.

Beginner friendly

🧘 Gentle Stretching

Try gentle shoulder, arm and leg movements if they are comfortable.

Duration: Short sessions with rest whenever needed.

Flexible activity

🏋️ Chair-Based Movement

Seated arm movements and gentle mobility exercises can be alternatives to standing activities.

Duration: Adjust to comfort and ability.

Seated option

🌿 Rest and Recovery

Rest is an important part of overall wellness. Take breaks whenever you need them.

Recovery

♿ Accessibility Matters

Choose movements that match your mobility and health needs. Avoid movements that cause pain or discomfort.

If you have a health condition or disability, ask a qualified healthcare professional about suitable activities.

This is a general activity guide, not a personalised exercise prescription. Your activities should be adapted to your age, health and abilities. Stop if you feel unwell or uncomfortable.
